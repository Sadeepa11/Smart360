PART D · FLYWEIGHT PATTERN

Smart Street Lights

Share intrinsic fixture state across 50,000+ streetlights to eliminate JVM heap bloat and maximize scalability.

Every streetlight fixture shares identical physical specifications (LED model, wattage, Kelvin color, and a 2KB calibration curve). Unique extrinsic attributes (GPS coordinates, dimming level) are passed at runtime.
